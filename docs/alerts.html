Optimal Entry Timing & Buy Zones

🎯 Prime Entry (In Buy Zone): Price is 0% to +3.5% above pivot with volume confirmation. Optimal risk-to-reward window.
⏳ Armed (Coiling): Sitting 0% to -2.5% below pivot with volatility contracting. Set Buy-Stop Limit order.
🛡️ 50 DMA Pullback: High-momentum Stage 2 stock testing its rising 50-day moving average on low volume.
⚠️ Extended: Rallied >5% past pivot. High chase risk — wait for consolidation.

The 200-Day DMA (DMA200) Structural Rule

🔄 200 DMA Reclaim: High-growth stock crossing back above its 200 DMA on volume. Early turnaround entry with tight stop.
🛡️ 200 DMA Floor: Long-term institutional floor test on rising 200 DMA.
🛑 Avoid Below 200 DMA: Capital protection guardrail — avoid buying when price is below a declining 200 DMA.

Sector Tailwind, Headwind & Neutral Climate

🟢 Sector Tailwind: Stock operates in a high-momentum leading sector index (e.g. Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, Nifty Infra) in a confirmed uptrend (Score ≥ 55). Over 50% of a stock's momentum is driven by its sector.
🔴 Sector Headwind: Stock belongs to a sector index in a structural downtrend (e.g. Nifty Metal, Nifty Energy, Nifty Bank with Score < 45). High macro drag — exercise caution.
⚪ Sector Neutral: Sector index is range-bound or consolidating.
