What this page is for

India Research runs the full NSE universe through a 3-phase fundamental + technical funnel to surface quality mid-caps with a live technical catalyst. This is a research/screening page, not a same-day trade trigger — for exact entry timing use Triggers or Breakout GEN2. Best used to build a watchlist of fundamentally sound compounders, then wait for the market to confirm with price action.

How the 3-phase funnel works

Phase 1 — Quality & Survival Sieve: Market cap ₹500–10000 Cr, ROE ≥15% (ROCE proxy), Debt/Equity <0.5.

Phase 2 — Growth Engine: 5Y EPS CAGR ≥18%, EBITDA margin ≥15%, Promoter holding ≥50%. Survivors form the Fundamental Watchlist.

Phase 3 — Technical Catalyst: price > EMA50 > SMA200 (confirmed uptrend) AND today's volume ≥1.5× the 20-day average. Only stocks clearing all 3 phases move to Active Breakouts; everyone else stays on the Watchlist, awaiting the trigger.

Column & badge glossary

Vol / 20d Avg: today's volume ÷ 20-day average — the Phase 3 trigger metric.

⏳ Awaiting Breakout: passed Phases 1+2 but the technical trigger hasn't fired yet — watch, don't buy yet.

ROE, EPS 5Y CAGR, D/E, Promoter%: the Phase 1/2 quality and growth filters, shown so you can see how comfortably each stock cleared the bar.

A background 0–100 composite (30% ROE + 30% EPS growth + 20% balance sheet + 20% promoter holding) feeds the Confluence/Triggers overlay but is not shown as a column here.

Caveats

Fundamentals refresh from the Tickertape screener each run; trend/volume come from Yahoo Finance daily bars (295-day lookback) and can lag intraday moves. Small/mid-caps here may be less liquid than large-caps — size positions accordingly. Not financial advice; verify independently before acting.
