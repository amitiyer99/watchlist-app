What this page is for

Multibagger Blueprint ranks NSE small/mid-caps (₹500–15,000 Cr) by a 6-factor MBF Score — a research/discovery screener for long-horizon compounders, not a same-day trade trigger. Confirm entry timing on Triggers or Breakout GEN2 before buying.

How the score works

MBF Score v3 (0–100) sums 6 factors: Earnings Engine (max 25) + Capital Quality (max 20) + Balance Sheet Fortress (max 18) + Valuation Discipline (max 12) + Price Momentum (max 15) + Smart Money (max 10).

Filters: 40+ = candidate, 55+ = solid, 65+ = High Conviction, 75+ = top tier. A liquidity floor (adv20 ≥ ₹2 Cr/day) is applied before scoring — illiquid names never appear here at all, because paper edge on thin stocks is fake once impact cost is priced in.

Factor & badge glossary

E Earnings Engine: 5Y EPS/revenue CAGR + earnings acceleration, minus an accruals penalty (⚠️) when strong EPS growth is not cash-backed by positive FCF.

Q Capital Quality: ROE, FCF yield, margin expansion.

F Balance Sheet Fortress: Debt/Equity, interest coverage (banking gets a neutral score — different capital norms).

V Valuation: PEG ratio, EV/EBITDA.

M Price Momentum: RS Rank, Jegadeesh-Titman 1Y/6M/1M composite, price vs 200-SMA.

S Smart Money: promoter holding + 3M change, FII/MF combo.

Badges (do not affect score): 🔥 Compounding Machine · 💎 Deep Value · 🚀 Discovery Zone · ⚡ Accelerating Growth · 💰 FCF Champion · 🛡️ Fortress · 📈 Momentum Leader.

Caveats

Fundamentals come from the Tickertape screener; liquidity (adv20) and momentum inputs come from Yahoo Finance daily bars. Small/mid-caps carry higher volatility and wider spreads than large-caps — size positions accordingly. Not financial advice; verify independently before trading.
