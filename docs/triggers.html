The trade, in one line

Entry → buy near here. Stop → set it the moment you buy, this is the whole risk plan. Timing — the two clocks

🕒 fresh / aging / stale shows how long a name has been an active trigger. Breakouts are best taken while fresh — the buy-by date is roughly 7 days (≈5 trading sessions) from when it first appeared. After that you're chasing an extended move.

⏳ weeks to target is a rough ATR-based estimate (assumes ~0.4×ATR of favourable drift per day), not a deadline. Exits are condition-driven — a stop, target, or trailing-stop hit — never a fixed calendar date. Once you enter, the exit engine manages trailing stops and flags a review if the trade stalls past ~60 days.
