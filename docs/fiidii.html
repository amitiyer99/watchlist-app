What this shows

Stocks where institutions bought via NSE-disclosed bulk & block deals in the last 90 days. Buyers are classified FII (foreign) or DII (domestic) from the disclosed client name. FII + DII = both bought (strongest signal), then FII / DII single-side.

Honest caveats

Bulk/block deals capture only large disclosed transactions (>0.5% of equity), not routine accumulation — a high-signal subset, not full holdings. FII/DII tagging is a name heuristic (unmatched buyers are ignored). The authoritative "increased holdings" is the quarterly shareholding filing (4×/year). Not investment advice.
