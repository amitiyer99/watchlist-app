What this shows

14 NSE sector indices (Nifty Bank, IT, Auto, Pharma, FMCG, Metal, Realty, etc.) ranked by a composite Trend Score, using 5 years of price history vs the Nifty 50 benchmark. Your own watchlist stocks are matched into their sector below each card.

Trend Score (0-100)

SMA position (30pts, is price above its 50/200-day average and is that average rising) + relative strength vs Nifty (25pts) + momentum (25pts) + RSI (20pts) — then nudged by the RRG quadrant below.

🔥 Strong Uptrend ≥70   📈 Uptrend ≥55   ➡️ Neutral ≥45   📉 Downtrend ≥30   ⬇️ Strong Downtrend below.

RRG rotation quadrant

Relative Rotation Graph — an early-rotation lens layered on top of the score. 🌀 Improving 🔥 means momentum is turning up before relative strength has caught up (often the best entry point). 🌀 Leading already outperforming and still rising. 🌀 Weakening outperforming but fading. 🌀 Lagging underperforming and losing momentum.

Returns, RS & RSI

Ret is the sector index's own price return over that window. RS is that return minus Nifty's return over the same window — positive means the sector is beating the index. RSI(14) is a momentum gauge (above 70 overbought, below 45 bearish).
