What this page is for

APEX Convergence Scout ranks the top 300 NSE stocks by a 5-pillar fundamental + technical composite. It is a research/ranking screener for shortlisting quality growth compounders — not a same-day trade trigger. Confirm exact entry timing on Triggers or Breakout GEN2 before buying.

How the score works

5 pillars, 20 pts each (100 max) + a +5 Convergence Bonus when all 5 pillars score ≥12/20 at once.

Elite ≥80 · Strong ≥65 · Aligned ≥50 · Misaligned below 50.

Action: 🟢 BUY needs total≥70 + Stage 2 uptrend + VCP pattern confirmed. 🔵 BUILD needs total≥65 + Stage 2. 🟡 WATCH needs total≥50. Earnings within 7 days demotes BUY→BUILD and BUILD→WATCH (gap-risk gate).

Pillar & column glossary

P1 Capital Quality (max 20): ROE, FCF yield, Debt/Equity, interest coverage.

P2 Growth Engine (max 20): 5Y EPS/revenue CAGR, EPS acceleration vs trend, margin expansion — penalised if 1Y EPS growth decelerates below 60% of the 5Y trend.

P3 Valuation Discipline (max 20): PEG ratio (P/E ÷ 5Y EPS CAGR, ≤0.8 is cheap), EV/EBITDA, FCF yield.

P4 Insider Conviction (max 20): promoter holding level + 3-month change, FII/MF combo, delivery-volume surge.

P5 Technical Setup (max 20): hard-gated to 0 unless Stage 2 confirmed; then VCP pass + RS Rating (convex-weighted).

2×2 Matrix: Safe Compounder = strong fundamentals (P1+P2+P3≥30) + ready setup (P4+P5≥20). Growth Bet = setup ready, fundamentals lagging. Waiting Room = fundamentals strong, setup not ready. Avoid = neither.

Caveats

Fundamentals come from the Tickertape screener (top 300 by market cap) with scorecard tags cached for 6 hours; the technical pillar needs breakout2-data.json (Stage2/VCP/RS) — without it, P5 is 0 for every stock. Not financial advice — always verify independently before trading.
